# Add diagrams to a study reading

You receive the Markdown reading of a study pack for the UPSC Vice Principal (GNCTD) exam. The candidate reads it on a phone.

Return the same reading with **1–3 diagrams** inserted where a picture teaches better than text: stage structures (e.g. 5+3+3+4), timelines, hierarchies, processes and flows of a procedure, who-does-what. Write each as a fenced code block tagged `mermaid`, placed right after the paragraph it illustrates.

Diagram rules: `flowchart TD` (top-down) or `timeline`; at most 8 nodes; labels of at most 5 words; no styling, colours, classes or HTML in labels; use only facts already in the reading.

Do not change any other text. Do not remove anything.
