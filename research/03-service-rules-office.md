## Gaps / notes

- **The DoPT Acts page has stale links.** https://dopt.gov.in/en/download/acts links CSMOP, the RRS and the Leave Rules to old URLs that now return 404. Use the URLs above.
- **CSMOP:** no consolidated "CSMOP 2022 with amendments" exists. Read the 2022 text together with the 11-03-2024 amendment OM. A DARPG OM on record-management practices (reported as Q-15011/1/2025-O&M, 01-08-2025) was mentioned in secondary sources, but no PDF was found.
- **Joining Time:** DoPT has no 2022+ consolidated PDF on its domain. The 21011/2/79 file is the DoPT-hosted base document, and the 12-09-2022 instructions exist only as HTML on doptcirculars.
- **LTC master circular (03-09-2025)** is HTML only on doptcirculars, with no PDF.
- **CCS (RP) Rules 2016:** the central gazette PDF is missing from doe.gov.in (the archive row is empty). The Puducherry republication is an official government PDF of the same text. The gazette itself is at egazette.gov.in (GSR 721(E), 25-07-2016).
- **CCS (Conduct) Rules:** DoPT's newest consolidated bare text is from 2015, so later amendments must be read from OMs. The DoPT conduct-OM listing is https://doptcirculars.nic.in/Default.aspx?URL=7aTU42EPuALu
- **Commercial books (no links):** Swamy's *Compilation of FR & SR Part I – General Rules*; Swamy's *Compilation of CCS Leave Rules*; Swamy's *Handbook*; Muthuswamy & Brinda (Swamy Publishers). Use them for annotated GoI decisions.
- The ISTM PRAVEENTA set (Office Procedure, Noting, Records, RTI, Leave, CCA) is the most exam-friendly free material, but it dates from about 2023. Check it against the 2024–25 DoPT texts above.
- A draft Delhi School Education (Amendment) Rules notification dated 10-08-2026 (omitting rule 44(3) and rule 50(ii), on opening new schools) was reported only on a non-official site. Check the Delhi Gazette to see whether it has been finalised.
