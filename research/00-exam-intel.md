### Scheme of the CRT (Appendix-I, quoted)
- "The test will be of two hours duration"
- "All questions will carry equal marks."
- Objective type, multiple choice.
- Medium: both English and Hindi.
- "Every wrong answer will carry a deduction of one-third of the marks assigned to that question." Unanswered questions carry no penalty.
- **Maximum 300 marks.** The official text does not state the number of questions.
- **Interview** is out of 100 marks. Minimum suitability: General/EWS 50, OBC 45, SC/ST 40. Candidates must reach their category's minimum at **both** the RT and the interview. The RT minimum is "decided by the Commission on case to case basis".

### Official syllabus (Appendix-I (II), quoted verbatim)
> The syllabus of the Test broadly comprises the following topics:-
> i. General Knowledge including Contemporary social, economic and cultural issues
> ii. Hindi and English language skills
> iii. Reasoning ability and Quantitative Aptitude
> iv. Educational policies & Educational measurement and evaluation
> v. Management and Financial administration
> vi. Office Procedure
> vii. Pedagogy in Education
> viii. Digital Literacy.

### Official topics compared with the candidate's list (BRIEF areas 1–7)

| Official topic | Covered by candidate area | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| i. GK incl. contemporary social, economic, cultural issues | 7 (partly) | **Mostly NOT covered.** Area 7 is current affairs focused on education. Static GK is missing: polity/Constitution, history and culture, geography, economy, science, and social and economic issues |
| ii. Hindi and English language skills | none | **NOT in list.** Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and usage in both languages |
| iii. Reasoning ability and Quantitative Aptitude | none (the "aptitude" in area 3 is the psychological construct) | **NOT in list.** Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, arithmetic, data interpretation |
| iv. Educational policies & educational measurement and evaluation | 1, 2, 3 | Covered. Add the formal side of measurement: reliability, validity, norm- vs criterion-referenced tests, item analysis, basic statistics, CCE/CBA, PARAKH |
| v. Management and Financial administration | 6 (GFR, procurement, FR, pay fixation) | **Partly.** School/educational management and leadership, planning, and budgeting/DDO functions are not listed. The **Delhi School Education Act & Rules 1973** are not listed either |
| vi. Office Procedure | 4 (and 5, 6) | Covered |
| vii. Pedagogy in Education | 3 | Mostly covered. Add teaching methods and approaches (constructivist, experiential, art- and sport-integrated), inclusive pedagogy, and lesson planning |
| viii. Digital Literacy | none | **NOT in list.** Computer and internet basics, MS Office, cyber safety, e-governance, ICT in education (DIKSHA, PM eVIDYA, SWAYAM, UDISE+) |

**Biggest new blocks:** (ii) language, (iii) reasoning and quant, (viii) digital literacy, and static GK in (i). These four topics probably carry a substantial share of the 300 marks.

### Commonly recommended books (title / author / publisher only; buy or borrow legitimately)
- **Office procedure and service rules:** Swamy's *Manual on Office Procedure*; Swamy's *Compilation of CCS Leave Rules*; Swamy's *Compilation of CCS (CCA) Rules* and *CCS (Conduct) Rules*; Swamy's *Compilation of FR & SR*; Swamy's *General Financial Rules 2017* (Swamy Publishers). Use these alongside the free official CSMOP (DARPG) and GFR 2017 (Dept of Expenditure).
- **Education psychology and pedagogy:** S.K. Mangal, *Advanced Educational Psychology* (PHI); S.K. Mangal & Uma Mangal, *Essentials of Educational Technology* (PHI); J.C. Aggarwal, *Essentials of Educational Psychology* (Vikas); J.C. Aggarwal, *Educational Administration, Management and Supervision* / *School Administration*; J.C. Aggarwal, *Essentials of Examination System: Evaluation, Tests and Measurement* (Vikas); Henry E. Garrett, *Statistics in Psychology and Education*.
- **Reasoning and quant:** R.S. Aggarwal, *Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations* and *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning* (S. Chand).
- **English and Hindi:** Wren & Martin, *High School English Grammar and Composition* (S. Chand); S.P. Bakshi, *Objective General English* (Arihant); Vasudev Nandan Prasad, *Adhunik Hindi Vyakaran aur Rachna* (Bharati Bhawan).
- **GK:** Lucent's *General Knowledge* (Lucent Publications); M. Laxmikanth, *Indian Polity* (McGraw Hill) for selected chapters; a monthly current-affairs magazine.
- Commercial "UPSC Principal & Vice Principal" guides and practice sets (Arihant, Kiran and others) exist. Their quality varies, so check them against the official syllabus and the 2022 papers.

## 3. Gaps / notes
- **Number of questions** is not in the official advertisement. Read it off the 2022 VP paper (P1 link above) once opened.
- **Application deadline extension to 17 Aug 2026** and admit-card status are unconfirmed on upsc.gov.in; they come from aggregators only. Check the recruitment-test "Notices" page: https://www.upsc.gov.in/recruitment/recruitment-test/notices
- No Hindi-version advertisement URL was verified. The English text is authoritative for the facts above.
- I did not open the cut-off PDFs (BRIEF rule: no full downloads). The 2022 VP RT cut-off is the best benchmark for a target score.
- **Access tip:** upsc.gov.in returns 403 to bots. Plain browsers work. Use the advertisement-number filter on archive listings.
- The 2022 VP answer key and Principal cut-off were uploaded in 2026, apparently after that recruitment's litigation or interviews finished. This does not affect their usefulness.
- **Syllabus gaps:** add language (Hindi + English), reasoning/quant, digital literacy, static GK, school management/leadership, and the DSEAR 1973 to the study plan (see the comparison table in section 1).
