# 06: Syllabus gaps (official topics missing from the candidate's list)

*Compiled 09 Oct 2026 for the UPSC CRT (Advt 51/2026, Vice Principal GNCTD) on 01 Nov 2026.*

This file covers official topics i, ii, iii, v (in part) and viii from Appendix-I of the advertisement (see `00-exam-intel.md`). None of these are in the candidate's areas 1–7. The "Covers syllabus area #" column uses the **official** topic numbers:

- **i** GK, including contemporary social, economic and cultural issues
- **ii** Hindi and English language skills
- **iii** Reasoning and quantitative aptitude
- **v** Management and financial administration
- **viii** Digital literacy

Where a resource also supports one of the candidate's own areas, I note it as "cand. #".

**How URLs were checked.** I sent `curl -I`, or a ranged GET of the first 8 bytes, with a browser user agent, and recorded the status and size.
- "200" means the link was verified on 09 Oct 2026. Size is the Content-Length.
- "**unverified**" means the server blocked bots, timed out, or has moved its site. Open those links in a browser.
- NCERT links point to the full-book ZIP (`<code>dd.zip`). The book's own page is `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?<code>=0-N`.

## Gaps / notes

- **No other education RT papers on upsc.gov.in.** The RT question-paper listings (current page and archive, back to 2019) contain no Education Officer, AEO, or Principal/VP paper for any other department. The 2022 VP and 2021 Principal papers in `00-exam-intel.md` are the only ones. The GAT papers in Section A are the best substitute for topics i, ii, iii and viii.
- **Site migrations broke many official links.** education.gov.in and dsel.education.gov.in moved to a new Next.js site, so old `/sites/upload_files/...` and `/sites/default/files/...` paths return 404. This affects the SMC Guidelines 2026, FMP-2024, the RTE Act PDF and the Delhi RTE Rules 2011 copy. NCSL-NIEPA also became an SPA, and old PDF paths there return an HTML shell. Open the DoSEL "new-updated-guidelines" page or the NCSL site in a browser to get the current file links.
- **Bot-blocked or timed out from this machine, so not verified:** nielit.gov.in PDFs, indiacode.nic.in, egyankosh.ac.in, samagra.education.gov.in, itpd.ncert.gov.in, cstt.education.gov.in, chdpublication, doe.gov.in, cybercrime.gov.in and i4c.mha.gov.in. These are official domains and are listed for that reason. Confirm each in a browser.
- **DPDP Act:** MeitY's old Act PDF link is dead. The TRAI-hosted Gazette copy works. The DPDP Rules were notified in Nov 2025 with phased commencement. The full-compliance date of about May 2027 and the consent-manager date of about Nov 2026 come from secondary sources.
- **NCERT downloads are ZIPs.** Several are large: Indian Art 200 MB, *Exploring Society* Class 6 211 MB. Chapter PDFs can be opened individually from `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?<code>=0-N`. NCERT is replacing books class by class under NCF-SE 2023: Class 9 now uses *Ganita Manjari* and *Understanding Society*. For GK, the older Class 11–12 books are still the most compact.
- **No free official Hindi grammar from NCERT.** Use the CHD spelling standard, NIOS Hindi (201), Rajbhasha/CHTI material, and the books listed.
- **India Year Book is not free.** No free official PDF of Yojana/Kurukshetra could be confirmed. Third-party "free PDF" reposts are of uncertain legality, so avoid them.
- **Process note:** one NCERT ZIP (`keec1dd.zip`, 24.8 MB) was fetched once to `/dev/null` while I was testing reachability. Nothing was saved. All other checks were HEAD or 8-byte ranged requests.
- **Suggested weighting** (inferred, not official). The 2022 VP paper is the only real evidence of the topic split. Until it has been read, a sensible split of prep time across the new blocks is: GK/current 25%, reasoning/quant 20%, English+Hindi 20%, digital literacy 15%, school management/finance 20%.
